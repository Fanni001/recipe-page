ROASTED SWEET POTATO
& CHICKPEA BOWL

HEALTHY & DELICIOUS

Vegetarian

A COLORFUL, NOURISHING BOWL PACKED WITH ROASTED SWEET POTATOES, CRISPY CHICKPEAS AND FRESH VEGETABLES.

30 minutes

550 calories

Beginner

WHAT YOU'LL NEED

INGREDIENTS

1 medium sweet potato

1 can of chickpeas

1 avocado

Fresh spinach

Olive oil

Paprika

Salt and pepper

FOR THE DRESSING

2 tbsp tahini

1 tbsp lemon juice

2 tbsp water

LET'S GET COOKING

INSTRUCTIONS

01 – PREHEAT THE OVEN

PREHEAT YOUR OVEN TO 200°C (180°C FAN). LINE A BAKING TRAY WITH PARCHMENT PAPER.

02 – PREPARE THE VEGETABLES

PEEL AND DICE THE SWEET POTATO INTO BITE-SIZED CUBES. DRAIN AND RINSE THE CHICKPEAS, THEN PAT THEM DRY.

03 – SEASON & ROAST

PLACE THE SWEET POTATO AND CHICKPEAS ON THE BAKING TRAY. DRIZZLE WITH OLIVE OIL AND SEASON WITH SMOKED PAPRIKA, SALT AND PEPPER. ROAST FOR 25–30 MINUTES, UNTIL THE SWEET POTATO IS TENDER AND THE CHICKPEAS ARE LIGHTLY CRISPY.

04 – MAKE THE DRESSING

IN A SMALL BOWL, COMBINE THE TAHINI, LEMON JUICE, WATER AND A PINCH OF SALT. STIR UNTIL SMOOTH AND CREAMY.

05 – ASSEMBLE YOUR BOWL

DIVIDE THE FRESH SPINACH BETWEEN TWO BOWLS. ADD THE ROASTED SWEET POTATO, CHICKPEAS AND SLICED AVOCADO.

06 – SERVE & ENJOY

DRIZZLE WITH THE TAHINI DRESSING AND SERVE IMMEDIATELY. ENJOY YOUR FRESH, COLOURFUL AND NOURISHING MEAL!

MAKE IT YOUR WAY

Sesame-free option

Replace the tahini dressing with a simple lemon and olive oil dressing.

Extra protein

Add grilled tofu or your favorite protein to make the bowl more filling.

Swap your greens

Use kale, arugula or mixed salad leaves instead of spinach.
